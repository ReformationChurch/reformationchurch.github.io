OUR VISION.
OUR MISSION.

Our commitment is to help people discover who God created them to be and how to bring His Kingdom into their everyday lives.

Our vision

RECLAIMING GOD’S
ORIGINAL INTENT
FOR MANKIND.

“An apostolic network of believers dedicated to empowering people to break free from the limitations of religion by equipping them to bring heaven to earth utilizing the principle of honor.”

“Thy Kingdom come, Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven!” Our ministry’s vision and mission are reflected in the way we teach, connect, and serve others.

The process

SEE. GROW.
EQUIP. EXPAND.

01 / EXPOSURE

Change what you see

Exposure is governed by the Kingdom principle of perception. Where you sit determines what you see, and what you see shapes what you believe is possible.

02 / EMPOWERMENT

Renew the mind

Transformation through renewing the mind, with a focus on God’s Word, meaningful partnerships, and learning from history.

03 / EQUIPPING

Discover your calling

Our desire is to equip people to discover God’s plan for their lives and use their gifts and authority to build others up.

04 / EXPAND

Multiply the impact

Through a lifestyle of honor, gifts are released, lives are changed, and the process repeats through the people we reach.
